# HR Analytics Dashboard – Atlas Labs Workforce Insights

## Project Overview

This project is an end-to-end HR Analytics solution developed in Power BI for a fictional technology company, Atlas Labs. The objective of the project was to design an interactive dashboard that enables HR leaders and business stakeholders to monitor workforce trends, employee performance, diversity metrics, and attrition drivers.

The project covered the full BI development lifecycle, including:

- Data modelling using a snowflake schema
- Data transformation and preparation in Power Query
- DAX measure development
- Interactive dashboard creation
- HR-focused exploratory data analysis (EDA)
- Report design and user experience optimization

The dashboard was designed to support data-driven workforce decisions by consolidating employee demographics, performance reviews, hiring trends, and attrition metrics into a centralized reporting solution.

---

## Business Goals

The primary business objective was to help Atlas Labs better understand:

- Workforce growth and hiring trends over time
- Employee attrition and the factors influencing turnover
- Diversity and inclusion metrics across age, gender, ethnicity, and marital status
- Employee satisfaction and performance review trends
- Departmental and job role workforce distribution
- Workforce retention risks and engagement indicators

The project aimed to provide HR stakeholders with actionable insights that could improve:

- Employee retention strategies
- Hiring planning and workforce forecasting
- Diversity and inclusion initiatives
- Performance management processes
- Organizational decision-making

## Key Insights Enabled

The dashboard enabled several strategic workforce insights, including:

- Atlas Labs had an overall attrition rate of 16.1%
- Sales Representatives experienced the highest attrition rates
- Frequent business travelers showed significantly higher employee turnover
- The majority of employees were aged between 20–29 years
- Technology was identified as the company’s largest department
- Demographic analysis revealed disparities in salary distribution across ethnic groups
- Employee satisfaction and work-life balance trends could be tracked longitudinally
- HR teams could proactively identify workforce retention risks using tenure and performance metrics
